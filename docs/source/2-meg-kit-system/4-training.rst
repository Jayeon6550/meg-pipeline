--------
Training
--------

A project owner can be trained by the MEG Lab scientists to become an authorized operator.
Throughout the training sessions, potential users will learn about operational protocols, emergency procedures, and safety requirements, as well as the steps that must be followed before and after MEG data acquisition.

Training Procedure
------------------

1. **Request for Training**

   Send an email request for training to: `nyuad.meg@nyu.edu <mailto:nyuad.meg@nyu.edu>`_

   Please include details about your project and proposed timelines.

2. **Download the MEG Lab Manual**

   Download the PDF version of the `MEG Lab Manual <https://github.com/BioMedicalImaging-Core-NYUAD/neurowaves-lab-documentation/releases/download/v-report-docs/meg-lab-manual.pdf>`_ for reference throughout your training.

3. **Introductory Session**

   A one- to two-hour introductory session will be scheduled to familiarize you with MEG operations and safety procedures.

4. **Follow-up Session**

   After completing the first session, arrange a follow-up session with a volunteer participant to gain hands-on practice with all procedures.

5. **Observation Period**

   Once both sessions are completed, it is recommended that you shadow a researcher conducting an approved study.
   This will help you understand participant preparation and the safe acquisition of MEG data.
   Please remain respectful and considerate of both the researcher and their participant during these observation sessions.

6. **Competency Assessment**

   When you feel confident with all aspects of MEG data acquisition, contact `nyuad.meg@nyu.edu <mailto:nyuad.meg@nyu.edu>`_ to arrange an assessment.

7. **Authorization**

   Upon successful completion of the assessment, and once both the MEG Lab scientist and the trainee are satisfied, the trainee will be authorized to independently acquire MEG data as a registered user.
   Access to the MEG Lab (A2-008) and all relevant calendars will then be granted.

8. **Additional Training (if needed)**

   If the MEG Lab scientist or trainee feels further preparation is needed, additional training sessions can be arranged.
